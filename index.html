01 / KNOW THE ENEMY

Common cyber threats

Cybercriminals use many tricks to steal information, money, or access. Recognizing the warning signs is your first layer of defense.

🎣
01

Phishing

Fake emails, texts, or websites designed to trick you into revealing passwords or personal information.

🦠
02

Malware

Malicious software such as viruses, spyware, and ransomware that can damage or control your device.

🔑
03

Password Attacks

Attackers try stolen, guessed, or reused passwords to break into your accounts.

👤
04

Identity Theft

Someone uses your personal information to impersonate you or access your accounts.

02 / BUILD YOUR DEFENSE

Everyday safety habits

Small actions create strong protection. Make these habits part of your everyday digital routine.

01
🔐

Use strong, unique passwords

Create long passwords or passphrases that are difficult to guess. Never reuse the same password across important accounts.

✓ 12–16+ characters✓ Unique for each account✓ Password manager recommended

04 / GET HELP & REPORT

Something happened online?

Don’t stay silent. If you experience a scam, hacking, cybercrime, or privacy violation, use the appropriate official reporting channel.

🚨
If money, passwords, or accounts are involved, act quickly.

Save screenshots, usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, transaction details, URLs, and other evidence before deleting anything.
